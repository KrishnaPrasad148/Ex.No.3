# Use Case Selected

## Topic: Artificial Intelligence in Healthcare

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is transforming healthcare by improving disease diagnosis, medical research, patient monitoring, and treatment recommendations. AI-based systems help doctors analyze large amounts of medical data and provide accurate predictions.

---

# 1. Straightforward Prompting

## Prompt:

**"Explain the role of Artificial Intelligence in healthcare in one paragraph."**

## Generated Response:

Artificial Intelligence in healthcare helps improve medical diagnosis, treatment planning, and patient care. AI algorithms can analyze medical images, detect diseases at early stages, predict health risks, and assist doctors in making better decisions. It also supports drug discovery, virtual health assistants, and personalized medicine, making healthcare services faster and more efficient.

---

# 2. Tabular Format Prompting

## Prompt:

**"Compare traditional healthcare systems and AI-based healthcare systems in a table."**

## Generated Response:

| Feature | Traditional Healthcare System | AI-Based Healthcare System |
|---------|------------------------------|----------------------------|
| Diagnosis | Depends mainly on doctor's analysis | Uses AI algorithms for faster analysis |
| Data Processing | Manual processing of patient records | Automated processing of large datasets |
| Accuracy | Depends on human expertise | Provides predictive and data-driven insights |
| Decision Making | Based on medical experience | Supported by AI recommendations |
| Patient Monitoring | Requires regular human supervision | Enables continuous monitoring using smart devices |

---

# 3. Preceding Question Prompting

## Prompt:

**"Why is Artificial Intelligence becoming important in healthcare? Explain how AI improves medical diagnosis and patient treatment."**

## Generated Response:

Artificial Intelligence is becoming important in healthcare because it can process large amounts of medical information quickly and accurately. AI improves diagnosis by identifying patterns in medical images, laboratory reports, and patient histories. It helps doctors detect diseases earlier and provides personalized treatment suggestions based on individual patient conditions.

---

# 4. Missing Word Prompting

## Prompt:

**"Artificial Intelligence in healthcare helps doctors by analyzing medical ______ and predicting diseases."**

## Generated Response:

**Artificial Intelligence in healthcare helps doctors by analyzing medical data and predicting diseases.**

# Evaluation of Prompting Techniques

| Prompt Type | Evaluation |
|-------------|------------|
| Straightforward Prompt | Produces simple and direct answers suitable for basic information retrieval. |
| Tabular Format Prompting | Organizes complex information clearly and improves comparison between concepts. |
| Preceding Question Prompting | Provides detailed responses by adding context before the main question. |
| Missing Word Prompting | Helps complete incomplete information and tests model understanding. |

---

# Advantages of Different Prompting Techniques

- Straightforward prompts are easy to create and provide quick responses.
- Tabular prompts improve readability and organization of information.
- Preceding questions provide better context and generate detailed explanations.
- Missing word prompts improve understanding of key concepts.
